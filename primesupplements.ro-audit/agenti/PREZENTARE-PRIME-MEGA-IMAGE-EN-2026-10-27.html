Who we are

A Romanian sports nutrition brand from Sibiu.

Prime Supplements was born in the weight room and grew online, on primesupplements.ro, and in gyms across Romania. The products are made in the European Union, with Romanian labels and only EU-authorised health claims.

  • 55+ products: protein, creatine, amino acids, pre-workout, vitamins and minerals, functional foods, accessories
  • 100+ blog articles explaining each product, the dose and when to take it
  • Partner gyms with a Prime shelf and a dedicated sales agent
  • Active community on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube
