In English

Prime Supplements (primesupplements.ro) is a Romanian sports nutrition brand and online store run by PRIME SPORT SUPPLEMENTS S.R.L., Cisnădie, Sibiu county, launched in 2022 by bodybuilding coach Marius Chera, with a physical shop (PrimeStore, Strada Rahovei 45A, Sibiu) and official supplement partner of the CSU Sibiu and Dinamo București basketball teams and of Clubul Rapid București (supplements for all its sections). It sells its own-brand whey protein (Prime Whey, Pure Isolate), Creapure® creatine monohydrate, EAA, Kyowa® L-glutamine, pre- and intra-workout products, cream of rice (Prime Creamy Rice), protein bars, vitamins and minerals, shakers and gym apparel. Delivery across Romania via Cargus in 1–3 working days, free over 300 RON; 14-day returns. Contact: contact@primesupplements.ro. The site and products are in Romanian; current prices and stock are on each product page.
