## Score
**36/100.** Product metadata is missing on more than half the catalog (105/185 no SEO title, 104/185 no meta, 79 with no meta tag at all), the title template carries no brand, the homepage H1 is the logo, 9/23 collections and 11/11 pages have no SEO fields, and the 354-article blog is reachable only through a 33-page prev/next pagination while 2582 of its links point at the myshopify.com hostname.

- Article-to-article linking exists (1474 links, median 3 outbound per article), and article anchors are descriptive titles (0 generic "click aici" anchors).
- `<html lang="ro">` is correct on all pages; paginated titles get a "– Pagina N" suffix so they are not exact duplicates.

## Findings

### ONPAGE-01 — 105/185 products have no SEO title, so `<title>` is the raw product name
- **Severity:** High
- **Evidence:** `$R/shopify/products-all.json`: `seo_title == ""` on 105 rows (16 supplements/accessories, 89 MNX apparel). Rendered examples (`$R/parsed/pages_product.json`, `pages_products_turmeric-extract-80-caps.json`, `pages_products_mnx-classic-t-shirt-golden-era-blue.json`): `<title>Baton proteic căpșuni</title>` (21 chars), `<title>Turmeric extract 80 caps</title>` (24), `<title>MNX classic T-shirt Golden Era, blue</title>` (36). Template `$R/theme/layout__theme.liquid` line 20: `<title>{% if page_title == blank %}{{ shop.name }}{% else %}{{ page_title }}…</title>` adds nothing.
- **Affected scope:** 105 product URLs (×2 with `/ro-hu/`). 16 effective titles are under 25 chars.
- **Recommendation:** Bulk-write `SEO title` in Admin (CSV/Matrixify or `productUpdate` with `seo.title`) using: supplements `"{Produs} {gramaj/porții} – {beneficiu principal}"` (brand appended by the template, see ONPAGE-03), e.g. `Baton proteic căpșuni 55g – 20g proteină, fără zahăr adăugat`; apparel `"{Tip articol} MNX {model} {culoare} – {material}"`, e.g. `Tricou MNX Golden Era albastru – tricou de sală din bumbac`. Keep ≤ 55 chars before the suffix.
- **Falsifiability:** After the fix, `products-all.json` rebuilt from Admin shows `seo_title` non-empty on 185/185 and no `<title>` under 30 chars in a crawl.
- **Leading indicator:** GSC → Pages report: impressions and CTR for the 105 URLs within 4-6 weeks; `site:primesupplements.ro "MNX"` SERP snippets show the new titles.
- **Effort:** M (content writing for 105 items; the write itself is S)
- **Confidence:** 0.95

### ONPAGE-03 — The title template adds no brand suffix and the 80 hand-written titles use three different brand spellings
- **Severity:** High
- **Evidence:** `layout__theme.liquid` line 20 renders `{{ page_title }}` only. Only 33/185 effective product titles contain the brand (18%). Among the 80 set SEO titles: `| PRIME Supplements` ×16, `| PrimeSupplements` ×15 (Frutilu range), `| Prime Supplements` ×1, 47 with no brand; articles use `- PRIME Supplements` (lcarnitine) and 3/335 contain the brand; collections `| Prime Supplements` ×1. Homepage title is `Prime Supplements - Hardcore Sports Nutrition` (English tagline, no product keyword).
- **Affected scope:** Site-wide (575 unique URLs; 1151 with `/ro-hu/`).
- **Recommendation:** Replace line 20 and lines 22-24 of `layout/theme.liquid` (on the duplicate theme) with:
```liquid
{%- liquid
  assign brand = shop.name
  assign seo_title = page_title
  if request.page_type == 'index'
    assign seo_title = 'Suplimente sportive și nutriție pentru sală – magazin online'
  endif
  assign probe = seo_title | downcase | remove: ' '
  assign has_brand = false
  if probe contains 'primesupplements'
    assign has_brand = true
  endif
  assign meta_desc = page_description
  if request.page_type == 'product' and product.metafields.global.description_tag == blank
    assign cat = ''
    for c in product.collections
      unless c.handle == 'all' or c.handle == 'cele-mai-vandute' or c.handle == 'lansate-recent' or c.handle == '11-tva' or c.handle == 'frontpage'
        assign cat = c.title
        break
      endunless
    endfor
    assign meta_desc = product.title | append: ' – ' | append: cat | append: ' de la Prime Supplements. Preț ' | append: product.price | money | append: ', livrare rapidă în toată România.'
  elsif request.page_type == 'collection' and meta_desc == blank
    assign meta_desc = collection.title | append: ' – ' | append: collection.products_count | append: ' produse Prime Supplements. Comandă online, livrare rapidă în România.'
  elsif request.page_type == 'article' and meta_desc == blank
    assign meta_desc = article.excerpt | default: article.content | strip_html | truncate: 155
  endif
-%}
<title>
  {{- seo_title -}}
  {%- if current_page != 1 %} – Pagina {{ current_page }}{% endif -%}
  {%- unless has_brand %} | {{ brand }}{% endunless -%}
</title>
{%- if meta_desc != blank -%}
  <meta name="description" content="{{ meta_desc | strip_newlines | truncate: 158 | escape }}">
{%- endif -%}
```
Then normalise the 32 hand-written brand suffixes to none (the template adds it) so nothing reads "… | PrimeSupplements | Prime Supplements". Keep hand-written titles ≤ 55 chars.
- **Falsifiability:** Every `<title>` in a crawl ends with "| Prime Supplements" exactly once; no product page lacks a meta description.
- **Leading indicator:** Brand-query SERP titles consistent within one crawl; GSC branded impressions.
- **Effort:** S (theme) + S (bulk normalisation)
- **Confidence:** 0.95
